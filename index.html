WHY USE SMART PAINT?

Make better painting decisions before spending money.

Colours can look different depending on lighting, room size, wall texture and surroundings. Our visualizer helps you experiment before making a final decision.

  • ✓ Preview colours on your own room
  • ✓ Reduce the risk of choosing unsuitable colours
  • ✓ Save your favourite designs
  • ✓ Compare different possibilities

Visualization Notice

The visualized colours are approximate. Actual results may vary depending on room lighting, screen calibration, wall texture and other environmental conditions.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bringing paint decisions into the digital world.

Smart Wall Paint Visualizer is a frontend implementation of a virtual room paint and design preview system. It allows users to experiment with wall colours and designs before actual painting.
